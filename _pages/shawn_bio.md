# Role and Job Scope

## 🎯 Role
- Deliver AI drone product line for experimentation to mature TRL AI algorithms and models  
- Develop AI swarm systems tailored for ST Engineering business areas  

## 📅 Day-to-Day Responsibilities
- Translate user Concept of Operations (ConOps) into operational requirements and technical specifications  
- Develop and deliver drone technical specifications, verified through flight trials  

## 🧠 AI Algorithms & Models in Scope
- **Perception:** Convolutional Neural Networks (CNN), semantic segmentation  
- **Navigation:** Visual navigation, SLAM (Simultaneous Localization and Mapping)  
- **Planning:** External agents leveraging Vision-Language Models (VLMs) to execute drone flight
