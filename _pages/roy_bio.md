Dr. Dibyendu Roy is a Principal AI Engineer at ST Engineering, specializing in distributed AI-driven control architectures for cooperative heterogeneous robotic systems. He earned his Ph.D. in Electrical Engineering from Jadavpur University, where his research focused on decentralized formation control and adaptive navigation of swarm robotic systems.
Prior to joining ST Engineering, he served as a Scientist at Agency for Science, Technology and Research (A\*STAR), leading research in intelligent navigation and multi-robot collaboration for manufacturing and logistics applications. His work bridges artificial intelligence, control systems, and robotics, translating advanced research into deployable industrial solutions.
Dr. Roy has authored numerous publications in leading journals and conferences and holds multiple international patents across the US, Europe, Australia, India, and Japan. His research interests include multi-robot systems, decentralized control, AI-based planning, and physics-aware robotic automation for real-world industrial environments.
